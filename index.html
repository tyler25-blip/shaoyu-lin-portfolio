About

Design that feels intelligent yet deeply human.

I'm Tyler — a Taiwanese designer based in Munich, now studying Interactive Media Systems with an HCI focus at TH Augsburg. My path began in industrial design at Taiwan Tech, turned toward UX during a design exchange in Munich, and keeps leaning into the space where research, interaction, and craft quietly meet.

Across Taiwan, Germany, and cross-cultural teams, I've worked the full arc of the design process — from user interviews and empathy mapping, through rapid prototyping, to production-ready UI. I care about strategy as much as pixels: why something is made, and how it can feel effortless once it reaches someone.

Outside of projects, I like quiet trails, slow coffee, and the kind of objects that become friends over time.
