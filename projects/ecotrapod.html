Ecotrapod

A coastal-regeneration module that merges wave dissipation with the values and aesthetics of ocean sustainability.

Role
Industrial Design
Timeline
2024
Tools
Rhino · KeyShot
Scope
Concept · CMF
Key outcome

A speculative coastal module that reframes hard infrastructure as something that protects shorelines and reads as part of the sea, not against it.

01 — Context

Coastal defence usually means concrete that fights the sea.

Traditional wave-breaking structures protect coastlines but scar them — heavy, grey, and indifferent to the ecosystems they sit in. Ecotrapod asks whether shoreline protection can do its engineering job while also carrying the meaning and beauty of ocean sustainability.

02 — Approach

Form that dissipates waves and invites life.

The module's geometry is shaped to dissipate wave energy with minimal material, while its surfaces and voids are designed to host marine life rather than repel it. The result reframes hard infrastructure as a quiet act of regeneration — function and ecological intent in one form.

03 — Outcome

Infrastructure with a conscience.

Ecotrapod is a concept exploring how industrial design can hold environmental values without sacrificing engineering purpose — part of an ongoing personal interest in circular and sustainable design.

My contribution

A solo conceptual project — I developed the premise, the wave-dissipating form, and the CMF and visual language.
