Ecoshell Track

A running-track surface made from recycled oyster shells — coastal waste given a second life underfoot.

Role
Industrial Design
Timeline
2024
Tools
Material R&D · Rhino
Scope
Concept · Material
Key outcome

A material concept that turns discarded oyster shells into a durable, low-impact running surface — closing a local waste loop.

01 — Context

A coastline's waste is a city's missing material.

Oyster farming leaves mountains of discarded shells along the coast — a local waste stream that mostly goes unused. Ecoshell Track asks whether that calcium-rich material could replace part of the resource-heavy mix that goes into sports surfaces.

02 — Approach

From shell waste to surface.

The concept processes recycled oyster shells into an aggregate for a running-track surface — aiming for the grip and give athletes need while diverting coastal waste and lowering the material footprint. Circular thinking, applied where people literally meet the ground.

03 — Outcome

A second life underfoot.

Ecoshell Track is a concept in my continuing exploration of circular and sustainable design — finding value in waste streams that are local, abundant, and overlooked.

My contribution

A solo conceptual project — I developed the material premise, the application, and the sustainability narrative.
