06UIGraphic2023

ViewSonic

Globally-adapted UI and marketing visuals for ViewSonic and NOMVDIC premium projector launches.

Role
Graphic / UI Intern
Timeline
Jul — Dec 2023
Client
ViewSonic · NOMVDIC
Scope
Marketing UI · Localization
Key outcome

Launch visuals and UI for premium projector campaigns, adapted to user habits across different markets for the official site and Amazon.

01 — Context

One product, many markets, different eyes.

ViewSonic's premium projector sub-brand NOMVDIC sells globally, but a layout that converts in one market can fall flat in another. The internship brief: produce marketing materials and UI for the official site and Amazon that fit how audiences in different countries actually read and shop.

02 — Approach

Designing UI to local habits, not just one template.

I designed marketing UI and visuals aligned to user habits across regions — adapting hierarchy, density, and tone to improve clarity and cross-cultural communication — for premium campaigns including the NOMVDIC P2000UST-RGB 4K UST triple-laser projector.

03 — Outcome

Shipped campaigns across markets.

The visuals went live on the NOMVDIC site and Amazon listings, supporting premium projector launches — and gave me an early, practical grounding in cross-cultural UX inside a global hardware brand.

My contribution

As a graphic / UI design intern, I produced the marketing visuals and localized UI for NOMVDIC's official website and Amazon listings, working within ViewSonic's brand system.
